FAQ about WHOLESALE PRESS ON NAILS

Can wholesale press-on nails be reused?

 

Yes, most of the fake nails are acrylic press on nails which can be reused if you maintain them well and remove them carefully. But remember to move the old glue from the fake nails.

Can I custom-press on nails and OEM my own label？

Yes, you can customize your press-on nails by providing us with your pattern, and we can produce glue-on nails to your specifications. But for a custom order, there is a requirement of MOQ. Please contact us for details.

What is the MOQ for your wholesale press on nails?

For wholesale press on nails in the store, there is no MOQ, you can enjoy a wholesale price for just one piece. But the more you buy, the lower the price you enjoy.

What’s the main material of your stick on nails?

For reputable press on nail suppliers usually use medical ABS material for these sturdy nails, or acrylic material that can fit seamlessly with your natural nails.
